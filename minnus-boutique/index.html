Our Story

Where Craft Meets Confidence

Located on Minor Road, Eravimangalam in the heart of Nadathara, Minnus Boutique has built a reputation as one of Thrissur's most highly regarded ladies' tailoring and design studios.

We specialize in custom stitching and bespoke dressmaking, focusing on precise fit and complete customer satisfaction. From everyday wear to elaborate festive outfits, every garment that leaves our studio is crafted with care, attention to detail, and a genuine passion for the art of tailoring.
